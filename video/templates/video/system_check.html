On the setup screen, pick your camera and microphone and confirm you can see yourself and the mic meter moves when you speak. You don't need to "join."

Having trouble?

  • Allow camera & microphone when your browser asks. If you dismissed it, click the camera/lock icon in the address bar and set both to Allow, then reload.
  • Use a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. Avoid in-app browsers (opening from a link inside another app).
  • If the wrong device shows, pick the right camera/microphone from the dropdowns on the setup screen.
  • Use headphones to avoid echo, and close other apps that use the camera (Zoom, Teams, FaceTime).
  • For the smoothest call, use a strong Wi-Fi or wired connection.
