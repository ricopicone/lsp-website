What we do

The School is organized around teaching and the formation of analysts. Faculty lead seminars that read Freud and Lacan closely; members gather in cartels — small groups formed around a shared question — and in working and reading groups that carry the work forward between meetings.

Much of what we do is open to the public; the rest belongs to the ongoing life of members.
