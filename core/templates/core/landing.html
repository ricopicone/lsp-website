How we are organized

A school run by its students.

Following Lacan's founding gesture, the School belongs to those who study within it. Its committees, cartels, and seminars are carried by the members themselves—candidates, analysts, and scholars who share the work of its direction.

{# ===================== Orientation ===================== #}

What we do

Teaching is at the center. Faculty lead seminars that read Freud and Lacan closely; members gather in cartels—small groups formed around a shared question.

Formation follows two tracks—Analyst and Scholar—each with its own path through candidacy. {% if directory_count %}Home to {{ directory_count }} analyst{{ directory_count|pluralize }}, candidate{{ directory_count|pluralize }}, and scholar{{ directory_count|pluralize }}, t{% else %}T{% endif %}he School welcomes clinicians, candidates in formation, and scholars from neighboring fields. Much of what we do is open to the public; the rest belongs to the ongoing life of members.
