---
title: Logging in
summary: Two easy ways to sign in, with or without a password, and what to do the first time.
---

Your account uses the email address the School has on file for you, the same
one that receives School mail. There is no separate username.

## The easiest way: a sign-in link

On the [log in page](/accounts/login/), choose
**[Email me a sign-in link](/accounts/login/link/)**. Enter your email address,
and we send you a link; click it and you are signed in. No password needed,
ever.

A few things to know:

- The link works for **15 minutes** and for **one click**. If it expires, or
  you need another, just request a new one.
- If it doesn't arrive within a minute or two, check your spam folder for a
  message from the Lacanian School of Psychoanalysis.

## Prefer a password?

You can also sign in the usual way, with your email and a password.

**Setting a password for the first time:** most members' accounts were created
for them, so there is no password yet. Click
**[Forgot your password?](/accounts/password/reset/)** on the log in page,
enter your email, and follow the emailed link to choose one. The same steps
reset a forgotten password later.

## If something isn't working

- **No email arrives.** Check spam first. Then make sure you are entering the
  address the School has on file for you; that address is your login.
- **"This link has expired."** Sign-in links are single-use and short-lived.
  Request a fresh one from the [log in page](/accounts/login/).
- **You would rather log in with a different email address.** Once signed in,
  you can [change your login email](/accounts/email/change/); we verify the
  new address before switching anything.
- Still stuck? Write to
  [website@lacanschool.org](mailto:website@lacanschool.org) and we will get
  you signed in.
