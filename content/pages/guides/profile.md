Your profile is how colleagues, prospective analysands, and the public find and
recognize you across the School's site. It already carries what we imported from
the directory—this guide shows you how to make it yours.

## Editing your profile

Open **Edit profile** from the account menu (top right), or go straight to
`/accounts/profile/`. Everything saves together when you press **Save changes**.

### Your photo

Your photo appears as a **circle** in the directory, on event pages, and beside
anything you publish. Click **Choose photo…**, then drag and zoom so your face
and shoulders fill the ring—the guide overlay shows you the frame. We store a
clean square crop, so the same photo looks right everywhere.

Already have a photo you like? You can leave it. Use **Re-center / zoom** to
adjust the existing one without re-uploading.

### Your bio and details

Write a short bio in the first person. You can also set credentials, languages,
location (which places you on the Find-an-Analyst map), and the areas you work
in. Keep it to a few sentences—this isn't a CV.

## Who sees what

Beside each field is a small visibility control with three settings:

- **Public**—anyone, signed in or not.
- **Members only**—signed-in members of the School.
- **Private**—staff and you.

The master switch, **List me in the public member directory**, controls whether
you appear in the directory and the map at all. We list members by default so
people can find you, but it's yours to turn off.

## What to do now

Add a photo and a short bio, then save. That's enough to feel the difference—you
can refine the rest any time.
