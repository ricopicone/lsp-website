Every member of the School has a private meeting room: a browser video room of
your own, separate from the rooms used for seminars and events. Use it for
office hours, application interviews, committee conversations, and anything
else that isn't a class. Find it under **My LSP → Meeting room**, in the menu
behind your photo.

The room is for the work of the School. Please don't use it for clinical work
with analysands.

## The one rule worth knowing

**Nobody can be in your room unless you are.** That holds for everyone, whoever
they are and however they were invited. If someone opens your link before
you've arrived, they see a page saying the meeting hasn't started, and it lets
them in as soon as you're there.

So a link that gets forwarded or lands in the wrong inbox can't put a stranger
in an empty room of yours. It also means you should open the room at the time
you agreed, or your visitor will be left waiting on the doorstep.

## Opening your room

Go to **My LSP → Meeting room** and press **Open my room**. The room is always
the same one, so the link never changes and you can reuse it.

## Inviting people

On the same tab, under **Who can join**:

- **LSP members**—tick them in the list. Type in the box above it to filter by
  name. They'll get a notification with a link, and they sign in as themselves.
- **Anyone else**—type them in the box below, one per line. A name on its own
  is enough. You can also write `jane@example.com`, or
  `Jane Doe <jane@example.com>`.

If you give an email address, we'll send them the link. If you leave it out,
you get a link to copy and send yourself, using the **Copy** button beside it.
Either way they don't need an account, and they don't need to sign in.

You can add a short note that your guest sees on the way in, for example
"Our interview, Thursday at 2".

Invitations last a month. To end one early, press **Revoke**.

## Letting people in one at a time

By default, anyone you've invited walks straight in once you're in the room.
Tick **Let people in one at a time** in the settings below and they'll instead
wait on a doorstep screen until you admit them, which you do from inside the
call. Useful during office hours, when you'd rather finish one conversation
before the next begins.

## Recording

Recording is **off** by default in your room, and no one but you can turn it
on. If you switch it on in the settings, a **Record** button appears in the
call; nothing is recorded until you press it, and everyone sees the recording
indicator while it runs.

A recording made in your room is yours alone. Nobody else can watch it, not
even staff. To share one, download it and send it on.

## Office hours

If you teach a seminar or convene a reading group, you can tell members when
they can find you. In the settings, choose one of three:

- **Not shown**—nothing appears anywhere. This is the default.
- **Posted hours**—your note is shown, and any LSP member can come in while
  you're in the room.
- **By appointment only**—your note is shown, but nobody can join uninvited.
  You invite them when you've agreed a time.

Then write a line saying when, or how to arrange it. It's free text, so
"Thursdays 3-4pm Pacific" and "alternate Tuesdays after seminar, or write to
me" both work.

Your hours appear on the Workspace of each seminar or reading group you lead,
so your students see them, and on your directory page, where they're visible to
members only. They're never shown publicly.

Posted hours don't commit you to anything: the room still opens only when you
open it, and you can change or withdraw them at any time.

## Who can have one

Meeting rooms are for members of the School. Auditors, students, applicants,
and anyone with a free account don't get a room of their own, but they can be
invited into yours.

## If something goes wrong

Test your camera and microphone beforehand at
[Test your video & audio](/video/system-check/). If a guest tells you the page
says you haven't started the meeting yet, check that you're actually in the
room; it can take a few seconds to notice you've arrived.

For anything else, write to [website@lacanschool.org](mailto:website@lacanschool.org).
