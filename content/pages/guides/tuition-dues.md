The School is sustained by its members. Two things may apply to you—**dues**
(annual membership) and **tuition** (for members in training). Both are tied to
**active** standing: if you're on leave, resigned, or emeritus, neither applies.

You'll find everything in one place under the **Account** tab of
[My Formation](/formation/?tab=account), and you can always pay dues directly at
[the Dues page](/dues/).

## Dues

Dues are annual and **tiered by role**: pre-candidates pay the least, candidates
more, and full analysts and scholars the most. The exact amount for your role
and the current year is shown on the dues page—amounts are set per academic
year, so check there rather than assuming last year's figure.

**Who owes dues:** members on the analyst or scholar tracks (pre-candidate,
candidate, analyst, scholar) in active standing. Auditors don't owe dues.

## Tuition

Members **in training**—pre-candidates and candidates, on either the analyst or
scholar track—pay tuition each year, for **four years in total** (the years
don't have to be back-to-back). Once you advance to Analyst or Scholar, you no
longer owe tuition. The Account tab shows your progress toward the four
years.

### Recording your decision

Each year, record where you stand for that year:

- **Committed**—you intend to pay, and will choose how below.
- **On a payment plan**—you'll pay in installments.
- **Paid in full**—settled in one payment.
- **Skipping this year**—you're not paying tuition this year.

### Paying

Once committed, you can **pay in full** in a single transaction, or **set up a
payment plan**—either two installments (September and February) or nine monthly
ones (September through May). You then pay each installment as it comes due. When
the last one is paid, your year flips to *paid in full* automatically.

### "Covered by tuition" seminars

If you're current on tuition for the year—committed, on a plan, or paid in
full—seminars that the School covers for tuition-paying members are **free** to
you. At registration you'll see a one-click *covered by tuition* option, with no
payment step. If you've chosen to **skip** the year, you pay the regular fee.

## Donations

Anyone—member or not—can make a gift on [the Donate page](/donate/), any amount, any time. It's
always optional and separate from dues and tuition.

## Questions about your account

Amounts, what you've paid, and what's outstanding all live on the Account tab,
on one statement with a running balance. If something looks off, the treasurer
can help—reach out and they'll reconcile it. You can also report a payment or
fee from before this website there, and the treasurer will review it.
