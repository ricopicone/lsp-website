The School's teaching happens in seminars and special events—many open to the
public, some members-only. This guide walks through finding one and registering.

## Finding what's on

- **Program** (`/program/`) lists the year's teaching seminars.
- **Events** (`/events/`) lists everything else—special events, reading groups,
  and one-off gatherings.
- **Calendar** (`/calendar/`) shows it all on a month grid.

Each event has its own page with the description, faculty, sessions, and
pricing.

## Registering

On an event page, press **Register →**. You'll see the price tiers; the one that
matches your role is pre-selected. A few things worth knowing:

- **Sliding scale.** Some events let you choose your own amount at or above a
  floor—including, sometimes, zero. None are turned away for lack of funds.
- **Covered by tuition.** If you're a tuition-paying member and the event is
  covered, you'll see a one-click "covered by tuition" option—no payment needed.
- **Free events** skip payment entirely and confirm you on the spot.

Otherwise you'll be sent to a secure checkout. When you're done, you land on a
confirmation page and get an email.

## After you register

Your **access details**—the Zoom link, room, or materials—appear on the event
page and in your confirmation email once you're registered. You can view or
**cancel** a registration from its confirmation page; cancellations are refunded
automatically.

## Try it now

There's a free **Preview Seminar** set up for exactly this—register for it to see
the whole flow end to end, then cancel if you like. Nothing is charged.
