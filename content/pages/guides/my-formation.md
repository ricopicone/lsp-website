**My Formation** (`/formation/`) is your own place in the School, gathered on one
page. It has up to three tabs, each with its own link you can return to or share:

- **Formation**—your advisor and your advancement steps.
- **Account**—your tuition decision, dues, and a statement of every charge
  and payment (shown when it applies to you). See the
  [Tuition & dues](/guides/tuition-dues/) guide.
- **Groups**—the cartels, seminars, and other groups you belong to now and have
  in the past.

## Your advisor

When you're accepted into LSP you enter as a **Pre-Candidate** and choose an
**Advisor**. Your advisor supports you through your formation and **presents your
demandes to the Meeting of the Analysts**. You can choose or change your advisor
from the Formation tab; they're notified when you do. The School encourages you to
speak with a prospective advisor first, and, if you decide to change advisors, to
talk through your reasons for the change.

## The steps of formation

Formation advances one step at a time, and each step is decided by the **Meeting
of the Analysts**:

- A **Palimpsest** carries you from Pre-Candidate to Candidate.
- A **Passage** (analyst track) or **Traversée** (scholar track) carries you from
  Candidate to Analyst or Scholar.

When you're ready for a step, you **open a demande** from the Formation tab. You
can add a short statement of your readiness and link the **Work** you're
presenting (it lives in the Works catalog, not as a file here). Your advisor then
writes a recommendation and presents it to the Meeting, which decides. You can
withdraw an open demande at any time before it's decided.

The Formation tab also shows your **history**—the steps you've already taken,
drawn from your role over time, so your path through the School is all in one
place.

## What you do, and what the School does

You do the personal parts: choose your advisor, open a demande, write your
statement, link your work, and record your tuition decision. Your **advisor**
presents your demande, and the **Meeting of the Analysts** makes the decision.
When a step is approved, your standing advances automatically.
