Parlêtre is the School's members-only commons—part forum, part live chat—for the
ongoing conversation between gatherings. Find it at [Parlêtre](/parletre/).

## Channels

The board is organized into **channels**. Some are open to every member; others
belong to a committee, a cartel, or a working group and appear only to their
members. Channels come in three kinds:

- **Forum** channels hold threaded discussions—start a topic, others reply.
- **Chat** channels are a live stream of messages, like a group chat.
- **Video** channels are browser meeting rooms—join with one click, no app to
  install, and see who's already live from the channel list.

## Posting

Open a channel and type in the composer at the bottom. **Enter** sends;
**Shift+Enter** adds a new line. You can attach a file, and **@mention** someone
to notify them directly. Posts can be edited or deleted after the fact.

## Staying current

- The **bell** (top right) collects your mentions and replies.
- Each channel tracks what you haven't read and offers a **jump to unread**.
- You can **subscribe** to a channel for email digests, and—where it's enabled—
  reply to those emails to post back to the thread.

Some channels are **disappearing**: messages there are redacted a set time after
posting. They're marked clearly at the top.

## Try it now

Say hello in the **Welcome** channel—a quick hello so the room isn't empty for
the next person who arrives.
