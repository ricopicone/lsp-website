## Publications by members

Members of the School write across clinical, scholarly, and translation work.
Their books and articles are gathered in the School's [Works](/works/) catalog,
which members keep up to date through their profiles.

- [GIFRIC](https://www.gifric.com)
- [Psychoanalytic Forum of Colorado](https://www.psychoanalyticforumofcolorado.org)
- [Freud Lacan Institute](https://www.freudlacaninstitute.com)
- [Lacanian Compass](https://www.lacaniancompass.com)
- [Lacan in Ireland](https://www.lacaninireland.com)
- [Lacan Online](https://www.lacanonline.com)
