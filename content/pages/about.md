The Lacan School of Psychoanalysis was established in 1990 to conduct
education, research, and treatment for the benefit of the local community.
The School transmits Freudian and Lacanian psychoanalytic theory, offers
formation to clinicians, and provides clinical supervision.

Since 2019, LSP has operated as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit entity
incorporated under California law.

## Governance

The School is governed by a Board of Directors of seven members who serve
two-year terms. At least three of the directors must be Analysts of the
School. The Board sets policy and oversees the work of the School's
committees and staff.

The Programming Committee designs the annual seminar program and
coordinates the offering of seminars, reading groups, and other formation
activities. The School Staff handles day-to-day administrative work —
registration, treasury, communications, and member services.

## Membership and Roles

Members of the School belong to one of several formation roles, recognizing
distinct stages of training and contribution. The roles include
Pre-Candidate Analyst, Candidate Analyst, and Analyst of the School,
alongside the parallel Scholar track for those whose engagement is
primarily theoretical and pedagogical. A public roster is available in
the [Directory](/directory/).
