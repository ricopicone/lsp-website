title: Advisor Meetings
author:
---

Each member in formation has an advisor who accompanies their path through the
School. They meet at least once a year, an annual meeting is required, and as
often beyond that as the work calls for. The advisor helps situate a member's
analysis, study, and participation, and presents the steps of their formation to
the School, a point of contact between the singular trajectory of one member and
the common life of the community.
