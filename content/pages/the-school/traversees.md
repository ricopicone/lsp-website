A traversée is the advancement of a Candidate Scholar to Scholar of the School—the
scholar-track counterpart to the analyst's [Passage](#passages). Its name marks the
*traversal* the School's [Scholar-formation founding
text](/documents/scholar-formation-founding-text/) calls "foundational to the
Scholar of the School": from the split subject of the unconscious to the *objet
petit a*, the remainder at the end of analysis.

The path is long. After the palimpsest, the new Candidate Scholar undertakes a
singular Scholar Project—"a gift to the School"—sustained over four years, often in
a project cartel. When they have brought their analysis to its logical end and are
ready to speak about that project, they may request a Traversée presentation to the
assembled School, and so become a Scholar of the School. The Scholar-formation
founding text was written by Annie Rogers and Marcelo Estrada in 2022.
