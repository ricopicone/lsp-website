A passage—*Passage / Traversée*—marks a crossing in a member's formation. It
is the step by which a Candidate advances to Analyst or Scholar of the School,
the later counterpart to the palimpsest, which opens the earlier passage from
Precandidate to Candidate.

Like the palimpsest, a passage is at once a written work and a moment recognized
by the community: the Advisor brings the candidate's desire before the Meeting of
the Analysts, and the candidate is then scheduled to present the Passage at the
next Days of Assembly. The name's second term, *traversée*—a traversal, a
crossing-through—points past any examination toward what is at stake in the end
of an analysis: not a credential conferred from outside, but a position arrived
at and attested.
