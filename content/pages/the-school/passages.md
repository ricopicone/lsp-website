A passage is the advancement of a Candidate Analyst to Analyst of the School—the
analyst-track counterpart to the Scholar's [Traversée](#traversees). It follows
the palimpsest, which marked the earlier step from Precandidate to Candidate.

Like the palimpsest, the passage turns on desire and is recognized by the
assembled School: the Advisor brings the candidate's desire before the Meeting of
the Analysts, and the candidate is then scheduled to present the Passage at the
next Days of Assembly. What is at stake is not a credential conferred from
outside but a position arrived at and attested.
