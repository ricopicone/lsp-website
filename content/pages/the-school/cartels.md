title: Cartels
author:
---

A cartel is a small group—traditionally four members, plus a "plus-one" who
provokes and sustains the work—formed around a shared question. It is Lacan's
device for studying without a master: each member pursues their own product,
accountable to the group rather than to a teacher. After a time the cartel
dissolves, and its members permute into new ones.
