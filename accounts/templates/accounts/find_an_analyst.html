Find an Analyst

Welcome to the Lacanian School of Psychoanalysis' Find an Analyst page. If you are looking for a referral to begin analysis with a member of LSP, you are in the right place. If you are only looking to work with someone in person, please search your location for available clinicians using the map on this page first.

LSP's referral coordinator will use your answers to this brief survey to connect you with analysts whose availability is compatible with your own — while keeping your personal identifying information confidential. After receiving your responses, the referral coordinator will provide you with a list of available analysts and their contact information within two weeks. It will be up to you and your desire to contact any clinician or clinicians and make further arrangements to begin your work together, including decisions regarding payment and how often you will meet.
