Citation style

One citation per line, following these MLA-adapted rules: 1) quotation marks around article titles; 2) italicize book titles by wrapping them in *asterisks* (*Écrits* renders as Écrits); 3) periods after author and book names; 4) commas inside quotation marks after an article; 5) page numbers and publication dates in parentheses; 6) a period ending each entry.

Apollon, W. “The Dream in the Wake of the Freudian Rupture,” in *The Dreams of Interpretation*, (2007), Eds. C. Liu et al. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, (pp. 23-38).
Freud, S. *The Interpretation of Dreams*, Chapter VII, Volume V, The Standard Edition.
Lacan, J. “Beyond the 'Reality Principle',” *Écrits*, (2006). Trans. Bruce Fink. New York: Norton.
