Readings — guidance & citation style

Readings should include primary writings of Freud and/or Lacan. Secondary sources should enhance engagement with Freud and Lacan. To offer CE credits, include at least 3 references published within the last 5 years.

One citation per line, following these MLA-adapted rules: 1) quotation marks around article titles; 2) italicize book titles; 3) periods after author and book names; 4) commas inside quotation marks after an article; 5) page numbers and publication dates in parentheses; 6) a period ending each entry.

Apollon, W. “The Dream in the Wake of the Freudian Rupture,” in The Dreams of Interpretation, (2007), Eds. C. Liu et al. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, (pp. 23-38).
Freud, S. The Interpretation of Dreams, Chapter VII, Volume V, The Standard Edition.
Lacan, J. “Beyond the 'Reality Principle',” Écrits, (2006). Trans. Bruce Fink. New York: Norton.
