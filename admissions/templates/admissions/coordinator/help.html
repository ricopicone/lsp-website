This console helps you carry an application from arrival to decision. You shepherd each application — acknowledge it, line up the two interviewers, keep things moving, and record the outcome. The Meeting of the Analysts makes the actual admission decision; you record it here.

New to it? Open the Applications tab and press ✦ Guided walkthrough (top right) for a step-by-step tour, and Create training sandbox to practice safely — see Practice safely below.

  1. Submitted. Someone applies. They receive an acknowledgment (automatically, or you send it — see Settings).
  2. Interviewing. You invite available Analysts; the first two to agree are each connected with the applicant by email to arrange a time. They conduct the interview and file a short report.
  3. Decided. The Meeting decides; you record Accept (which admits the applicant as a Precandidate) or Decline. The applicant is emailed either way.

The dashboard

The Applications tab lists every application. Use the dropdown to switch between Open and All. Each row shows:

  • Ack. — whether the acknowledgment has gone out. A ✓ means sent; otherwise a Send button lets you send it now.
  • Interviews — how many of the two interviewer seats are filled (e.g. 1 / 2).
  • Reports — how many of those interviewers have filed their report.
  • Row actions — Invite / Re-invite the analysts, or Nudge any interviewer whose report is still outstanding.

Click an applicant's name to open their page, where all the controls live.

An applicant's page

  • Invite interviewers. Emails every available Analyst (those marked available or unknown for Application Interviews, drawn from the Analyst availability table) inviting them to interview. The first two to agree are automatically connected with the applicant — no assigning by hand needed.
  • Or add a specific interviewer. An override: pick an Analyst and connect them directly without waiting for a volunteer. The list is ordered availability-first.
  • Reports. Once an interview happens, record the date and a short report for each interviewer.
  • Decision. When the Meeting has decided, enter the effective academic year (for an accept) and any note, then press Accept & admit or Decline. The applicant is emailed automatically.

Automatic vs. by hand (Settings)

On the Settings tab you choose, for the acknowledgment and for interviewer invitations, whether they go out automatically on submit or wait for you to send them. Either way you can always send by hand from the dashboard. (The availability-reminder cadence there is shared with the Analyst Availability console.)

Wording of the emails (Messages)

The Messages tab lets you edit the subject and body of every email this console sends — the acknowledgment, the invitation, the introduction, the reminder, and the accept/decline letters. Placeholders like {name} are filled in automatically; the list of available placeholders is shown on each message.

Analyst availability

The Analyst availability tab is the table of which Analysts are available for each function (including Application Interviews) — this is what the invitations draw on. Overview shows coverage at a glance, and Reminder message is the note that asks Analysts to keep their availability current.

Practice safely — the training sandbox

To learn the workflow without contacting anyone, press Create training sandbox on the Applications tab. It adds a few practice applicants and Analysts, all marked 🧪 Sandbox. While you work a sandbox applicant, every email is redirected to you (tagged [SANDBOX]) — no real member is ever contacted.

  • On a sandbox applicant's page, Simulate analyst responses fast-forwards the interview stage — the practice Analysts agree and file reports — so you can reach the decision without any back-and-forth.
  • ↻ Reset sandbox returns the practice applicants to their starting state (and clears the walkthrough) whenever you want a fresh run.
