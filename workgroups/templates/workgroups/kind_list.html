A cartel is a small working group, usually four members and a "plus-one," formed around a shared question and dissolved when its work is done. Each member pursues their own line of inquiry; the plus-one attends to the group's dynamic rather than its content. Cartels are one of the School's core forms of study, and members may form or join one throughout the year.
